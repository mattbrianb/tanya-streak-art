# Your website: a quick guide for Tanya

**Your portal:** `https://<your-domain>/portal/`
Sign in with the password Matt gives you. Then tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, so it opens like an app.

## Add a new painting
1. Tap **Add a new painting**.
2. Tap **Add photos**. You can take a photo or pick from your phone. The first photo is the one people see in the shop; a close-up of the paint makes a lovely second photo.
3. Fill in the name, the size in cm (width × height), the medium and the price.
4. Choose **For sale**.
5. Tap **Save painting**. It's on your website straight away.

**Photos tip:** make the first photo the painting on its own, taken straight on in daylight. Your website then shows it hanging in a matching room automatically. Extra photos (close-ups, or the painting in someone's home) appear as thumbnails on the painting's page.

## When a painting sells
Go to **My paintings** and tap **Mark as sold**. It moves out of the Shop and stays in your Gallery with a "Sold" label, so people can see your past work and ask for something similar.

If you'd rather it disappear completely, open it and choose **Hidden**.

## Finished commissions
Paintings you've made for people, and originals that have sold, go on a page on your website called **Completed commissions** (under **Commissions** in the menu). Each one gets its own spread with its photo and story, one after another as people scroll. The 22 paintings you sent are already there: we gave them names and a short description, so open any of them to change the name, add the size, or replace the description with the real story.
1. On the home screen, tap **Finished commissions**, then **Add new**.
2. Add photos. The first is the one people see first; a photo of it hanging in its new home is a lovely second.
3. Add the name, size and medium, and a line or two in **The story**: who it was for or the occasion. First names only is best.
4. Choose **Show it**, then tap **Save commission**.

They also drift past slowly in a strip on your home page and at the bottom of the Gallery. The **Commissions** menu on your website gets its "Completed commissions" link as soon as the first one is saved. To take one off the website without deleting it, tap **Hide from website**.

## Your email list
- People join from the pop-up box on your website or from the bottom of every page.
- In **Subscribers**, tap **Copy all emails**. Then start a new email in Gmail and paste them into the **BCC** box, so nobody sees anyone else's address.
- At the bottom of every email, add: *Don't want these? Unsubscribe at https://<your-domain>/unsubscribe/*

## Settings
- **Commission prices:** what the website shows for Small, Medium and Large commissions.
- **Sign-up box:** turn the pop-up on or off, and change its wording.
- **Password:** change it any time.
