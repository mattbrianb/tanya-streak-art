How it works

  1. Send the details. Fill in the short form and it opens WhatsApp with your message ready to send.
  2. Talk it through. Tanya replies to chat about flowers, colours and size, and confirms the price.
  3. Book your spot. A deposit reserves your place on the easel; Tanya will let you know the amount and roughly how long it will take.
  4. Watch it grow. She sends progress photos, then it’s ready to collect in Durban North or be couriered to you.
