Meet Tanya

Tanya Streak paints florals in oils from her home studio in Durban North: roses in jam jars, hydrangeas, sweet peas and sunflowers, picked from the garden and the market.

She paints with a loaded brush and a palette knife, so the petals stand up off the canvas. Now and then she goes big, with mixed-media botanicals in acrylic, Posca and real gold leaf, like Miami Vibes at a full metre square.

Small pieces can come with a little display easel; larger ones arrive ready to hang.
